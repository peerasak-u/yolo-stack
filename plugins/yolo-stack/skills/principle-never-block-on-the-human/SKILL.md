# Never Block on the Human

The human reviews after the fact. On work that can be undone, decide, proceed, and show what you did.

**Why:** A question that could have been a finished draft makes the work wait for the human. A wrong choice on work that can be undone costs less than the wait.

**Pattern:**
1. Sort the action with the Autonomy lists in the **yolo-mode** skill and in the human's own `mode.md`.
2. It can be undone. Do it, then say what you did and why.
3. It is a choice between approaches. Pick one, and name the one you did not take.
4. It cannot be undone, or it is on neither list and you cannot tell. Stop and ask one specific question, with your recommendation.

What the work is for stays the human's call. How to carry it out does not wait for them.

**You skipped this if:** the reply ends with a question about work that can be undone, and doing the work would have answered it.

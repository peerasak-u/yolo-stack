# Chat

## What this source contains

- Real-time discussion of problems and decisions
- Group chats with clients or vendors
- Questions answered by a senior colleague that never reached a document
- Follow-up discussion that explains why something was revisited
- Direct messages (usually not searchable, scope accordingly)

Chat is often where the decision was made, especially for small changes that didn't warrant a document. It's also the most short-lived source. Messages get deleted, groups get archived, and search degrades over time.

## How to search it

Chat tools vary. Inspect the tool's schema first. If sign-in fails, stop and report the gap.

1. **Author-bounded search.** Messages from the item's author around the date it changed.
2. **Keyword search for the key terms.** Include misspellings, nicknames, and casual phrasings.
3. **File name and link search.** People paste the file or its link when they discuss it.
4. **Channel-scoped search.** Narrow to the team's channel, the project or client group, and any channel for problems or complaints.
5. **Thread traversal.** When you find a relevant message, fetch the whole thread. The decision often lives in the replies.

## What good evidence looks like here

- A thread where tradeoffs were debated ("I was going to use A but B is better because...")
- A message describing the problem the item prevents
- A question and an authoritative answer from the author or the person in charge
- A reference to a meeting where a decision was made
- A message relaying what a client asked for

## Common pitfalls

- **Retention limits.** Very old messages may be gone. If you find nothing before a certain date, note the retention cliff.
- **Unsearched direct messages.** Many decisions happen there. You'll miss them. Say so.
- **Jokes as "decisions."** Chat is casual. "Lol just do the thing" isn't a decision, even if the change followed it. Look for considered discussion.
- **Single messages out of context.** A message reads differently without its thread. Always fetch the thread.
- **Sign-in failures.** If the tool isn't signed in, stop. Don't make up findings. Report that chat wasn't searchable.

## What to return

For each relevant thread:
- Channel or group name
- Permalink or thread ID
- Participants
- Date range of the discussion
- The key quotes (verbatim) with attribution
